# Pack: services

Client-only category starter: a local service business site at `web/app/src/View/Services/`. The first starter of the `services` category (the successor naming scheme to the bot personas — no "ServiceBot"): the demo business is a remodeling contractor, but the shape fits any trade — plumber, electrician, landscaper, cleaner. The transformation is the proof; the quote and the call are the asks.

## What ships

- A multi-page trades site on the landing kernel's `sitework` register (the plan-table look: plan-grid work paper, stenciled uppercase display, safety-orange accent — `docs/landing.md` "Trades / contractor" blueprint):
    - **Home** — split hero with a live **Open / Closed** badge (the shared hours engine, `web/app/src/View/Landing/hours.ts`) and the two asks a trades site lives on: a quote CTA and a **click-to-call** button; a six-service card grid; a before/after teaser; the license line over a trust-metrics strip; a featured testimonial; the towns-served strip; a closing quote banner
    - **Projects** (`/projects`) — the signature section at full strength: every project as a **draggable before/after comparison** (the gallery kernel's `before-after` variant — pointer drag, tap, and keyboard arrows all work), each frame with a corner expand button that opens the pair **full screen in the lightbox** (after/before as adjacent slides), then the four-step process timeline
    - **Services** (`/services`) — the full service list with honest **starting prices** riding the `meta` slot, plus an FAQ that answers the real objections (estimates, licensing, permits, scheduling, payment)
    - **About** (`/about`) — crew photo beside the story with trust bullets, an uppercase credentials strip (license number, bonded & insured), and the review grid
    - **Quote** (`/quote`) — a detail form (name/phone/email/town/project/timing/message) delivering through the platform's managed forms pipeline (`formKey: "quote-request"`) — the owner gets an email and a dashboard entry with zero setup. The phone channel is a `tel:` link (click-to-call is how trades customers reach out); the email shows as copyable plain text (never a `mailto:` link)
- **Everything renders from `content.ts`** — business, hours, services, projects, testimonials, FAQ, quote copy: one typed file; no backend, no CMS
- Demo business: a coherent fictional remodeling contractor (Cedar & Stone Remodeling, Bend, Oregon) with five before/after project pairs, processed through `npm run image -- responsive` into `web/app/public/services/` — every image ships intrinsic dimensions and a WebP `srcSet`, so pages load layout-shift-free and phones never download desktop files
- Seventeen derived templates ride the same pages with their own seeds (see `packs/README.md` "Derived templates"):
    - on the base's `sitework` pages, re-inked by brand and nav: `services-landscape` (Juniper Ridge Landscaping), `services-painting` (Deschutes Painting Co.), `services-hair` (Copper & Ash, a one-chair color studio), and `services-makeup` (Velvet Hour Makeup)
    - on registers and skeletons of their own (each pins `landing.style` + `landing.pages`): `services-builder` (Tideline Builders, `tideline`), `services-hair-braids` (The Crown Room, `crown`), `services-landscape-native` (Wild Ground, `riso`), `services-makeup-counter` (Marisol Vega, `vanity`), `services-makeup-tidewater` (Tidewater Beauty Co., `daybook`), `services-makeup-noor` (Studio Noor, `jharokha`), `services-contractor-meridian` (Meridian Coastal, `horizon`), `services-contractor-hallock` (Hallock & Sons, `shingle`), `services-hair-maren` (Maren Atelier, `limestone`), `services-hair-sable` (Sable Atelier, `cognac`), `services-painting-swiss` (Shotgun Color Co., `paintchip`), `services-landscape-olivetta` (Olivetta Garden Studio, `plaster`: the seasonal garden journal), `services-landscape-koen` (Kōen Garden Studio, `basalt`: a numbered garden walk and the studio's seal), and `services-painting-casacal` (Casa Cal, `limewash`: the room stack home)
- When the pack is active it owns `/`, `/projects`, `/services`, `/about`, and `/quote`; otherwise the same pages preview under `/services`, `/services/projects`, ... (the services list previews at `/services/services` — the pack key and the page share a name, and the preview prefix wins)

Set [`../active.json`](../active.json) to `{ "key": "services" }` to make this pack the home surface.

## Register and special fields

Every field below belongs to a section _variant_, not to the register, so a remix to any other `style.preset` keeps it; the register only restyles it (docs/landing-content.md → "The registers" lists every preset and treatment). Text is click-to-edit and photographs Replace-able in the preview editor, and the content paths named are Content-panel slots in `catalog.json`'s `contentContract` (switches such as `home.hero.storefront` stay in code).

- **Registers**: `sitework` here and on the four original remixes; the restyled remixes wear `tideline` (`grain`, `hairline`, `mist`, services-builder), `crown` (`metallic`, services-hair-braids), `riso` (`two-ink`, services-landscape-native), `vanity` (`lacquer`, services-makeup-counter), `daybook` (`keepsake`, `hairline`, services-makeup-tidewater), `jharokha` (`filigree`, `grain`, services-makeup-noor), `horizon` (`panorama`, `hairline`, services-contractor-meridian), `shingle` (`engraved`, services-contractor-hallock), `limestone` (`alcove`, `hairline`, services-hair-maren), `cognac` (`ritual`, `grain`, services-hair-sable), `paintchip` (`colorblock`, services-painting-swiss), `plaster` (`journal`, services-landscape-olivetta), `basalt` (`stillness`, `mist`, services-landscape-koen) and `limewash` (`framestack`, `roomline`, services-painting-casacal).
- **Hero**: `home.hero.storefront` picks `split-media` (live badge, `credit`) or `full-bleed-media` (`home.hero.kicker`, `accent`, `credit`, `caption`, `coverLines` for the crown cover lines, `seal` for a studio's seal).
- **Home bands**: `home.featuredProjects` (the before/after teaser) and `home.proofMetrics` (the metrics strip; empty moves it to the about page) are filled here; `nowBuilding`, `journal` (showcase `stories`), `walk` (gallery `sequence`), `buildLog`, `species`, `palette`, `priceMenu`, `lookbook`, and `policies` ship empty (each drops its section) with the slots in place for an owner to fill.
- **The `stack` home** (`home.stack`, set only by services-painting-casacal): the hero room with `home.headline` as its line and no ask (`home.heroImage`), the rooms edge to edge at the hero's size with each caption written into the foot of its photograph (`home.stack.frames[]` image/caption, gallery `sequence` + `captionStack` `overlay-start`), and the closing line over the quote link (`home.stack.closing`, `home.stack.closingCta`; cta-banner `colophon` at the column's start). Projects, services, about, and quote keep their pages; unset, the home is the storefront or title-card home above.
- **Specials**: `palette` (`showcase` `swatches`; `palette.items[].color` is a `#rrggbb` content color), `priceMenu` (`pricing` `price-list`/`price-tags`, `priceMenu.groups[].items[]`), `lookbook`, `policies` (`feature-grid` `checklist`), `process`, `buildLog` (`steps[].duration` sets a step's minutes on the rail; an empty `label` lets the rail number the step), `species` (`showcase` `specimens`).

## Agent recipe: make it yours

- Change the business: edit `business`, `weeklyHours`, `serviceArea`, `landingCopy` (nav labels, kickers, banners), `home`, `about`, and `quote` in `web/app/src/View/Services/content.ts`. The license line renders in the footer, the metrics strip, and the about bullets — keep it real.
- Change the trade: the slots are trade-agnostic. A plumber swaps the services (water heaters, repipes, drain service), the projects (a repiped crawlspace reads as well as a remodeled kitchen), and the copy; the pages follow.
- Swap in real photographs: run each original through `npm run image -- responsive <file> --out-dir web/app/public/services --alt "..."` and paste the emitted media entry into the slot. Never commit raw camera files into media slots.
- Before/after pairs are the craft: shoot both frames from the **same angle** — the comparison divider only convinces when the room registers as the same room. Update a project's `before`/`after` together.
- Add a service or project: append to `services` or `projects` in `content.ts`; the home teaser (`home.featuredProjects`), the projects page, and the services grid all follow.
- Adjust hours: `weeklyHours` uses minutes since midnight per weekday; the hero badge ("Open — closes 5 PM") recomputes every render from the shared hours engine.
- Content tests guard the file: every image carries dimensions + alt text, service and project slugs stay unique, before/after pairs stay complete, and prices stay in the `priceNote` strings.

## Non-goals for this pack

- Deposits and project scheduling beyond the estimate-visit booking widget
- Auth / accounts, payments, invoicing
- Server-side state — the shipped site is fully client-side; quote requests deliver through the platform's managed forms pipeline (email + dashboard), no backend needed
