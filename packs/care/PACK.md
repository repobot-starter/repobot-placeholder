# Pack: care

Client-only category starter: a primary-care practice's site at `web/app/src/View/Care/`. First starter in the `healthcare` category — practice marketing made computable AND bookable: the business facts live in the content contract's `practice` domain, and the `appointments` domain (visit types x provider availability windows) projects into real capacity-1 booking slots on the managed booking domain (booking mode 2).

## What ships

- A five-page site on the landing kernel's `luxe-light` register worn in the practice's own calm teal (near-white ground, deep ink type, hairline rules, crisp elevation; `docs/landing.md`), with the full-width nav treatment (wordmark at the true left edge, links at the true right — deliberately apart from the centered/squared/inset navbars):
    - **Home** — split-media hero with a live "Open — closes 5 PM" badge computed from the contract's hours, the in-network insurance strip, a services grid, provider portraits, owner-curated patient reviews, the visit panel (map-ready address, phone, grouped hours), and the booking band
    - **Providers** (`/providers`) — four clinicians with credentials, portraits, and bios, then the story of the practice
    - **Services** (`/what-we-treat`) — the full services grid and the insurance strip (`/services` belongs to the services pack's preview route, which every checkout keeps)
    - **New patients** (`/new-patients`) — the first-visit guide as a timeline (before your visit, what to bring, the 45-minute first visit, records & refills)
- **Book** (`/book`) — **online appointment booking**: the AppointmentWidget projects each provider's actual weekly windows x visit types (new patient 45 min, follow-up 15, annual physical 30) into concrete bookable times. Live availability from the booking domain on a deploy; workspace simulation with the same provider-overlap rule, so double-booking is impossible in both worlds
- **Everything renders from `content.ts`** — the practice, the providers, services, insurance, hours, reviews, the new-patient guide, the appointment offering, and the content-driven sections (`promises`, `exhibits`, `menu`, `spotlight`, `faq`; each renders only when filled — the spotlight once it has a headline — and `home.directory` feeds the hero's directory aside): one typed file; no backend to run, booking rides the platform's managed booking domain
- The business facts resolve through the business-content contract (`repobot.content.json` `practice` + `appointments` domains — the Manage UI's write surface) over the code defaults, so the owner edits facts in Manage and the site repaints
- Demo practice: a coherent fictional clinic (Alder House Family Medicine, Bellingham, Washington) with four providers, eight services, ten insurance plans, Saturday hours, and calm natural-light photography processed through `npm run image -- responsive` into `web/app/public/care/`; every image ships intrinsic dimensions and a WebP `srcSet`
- Seventeen derived templates ride the same pages with their own seeds (see `packs/README.md` "Derived templates"):
    - on the base's `luxe-light` pages, re-inked by brand and nav: `care-therapy` (Stillwater Family Therapy, warm clay, centered masthead), `care-psychology` (Juniper Hill Psychology, slate blue, split letterhead), and `care-dental` (Waverly Dental, sky blue, inline card)
    - on registers and skeletons of their own (each pins `landing.style` + `landing.pages`): `care-direct` (Dr. Ada Okafor Direct Primary Care, `wayfinding`), `care-therapy-duet` (Duet Family Therapy, `groove`), `care-psychology-studio` (Miriam Castellanos, PsyD, `inkblot`), `care-dental-bright` (Bright Side Dental, `bubblegum`), `care-primary-magnolia` (Magnolia Concierge Medicine, `parlor`), `care-therapy-opentrail` (Open Trail Counseling, `trailhead`), `care-therapy-kindred` (Kindred Therapy Collective, `hearth`), `care-psychology-cambridge` (Cambridge Neuropsychology, `colophon`), `care-psychology-northlight` (Northlight Psychiatry, `boreal`), `care-dental-atwater` (Atwater Orthodontics, `terrazzo`), `care-dental-fernfinch` (Fern & Finch Pediatric Dentistry, `picturebook`), `care-obgyn-solana` (Solana Women's Health, `seaglass`), `care-obgyn-oakadobe` (Oak & Adobe Birth Center, `adobe`), and `care-obgyn-clearwater` (Clearwater Fertility, `fogline`) — the last two on the `stack` home
- When the pack is active it owns `/`, `/providers`, `/what-we-treat`, `/new-patients`, and `/book`; otherwise the same pages preview under `/care/*`

Set [`../active.json`](../active.json) to `{ "key": "care" }` to make this pack the home surface.

## The clinically-empty booking constraint (deliberate architecture)

Booking a visit asks for a **name, email, optional phone, visit type, and new/returning** — nothing else. No free-text reason for the visit, no symptom fields, no health questions, ever. The platform never holds medical information; the schema enforces it end to end (the widget's tests enumerate the form's every field, and the platform's booking door drops unknown fields). Marketing copy may say that patient health information never touches the site — it is true and the `/book` page says exactly that. Do **not** write "HIPAA compliant" (or any compliance claim) into template copy: the site avoids the problem by architecture instead of claiming certification.

## Register and special fields

Every field below belongs to a section _variant_, not to the register, so a remix to any other `style.preset` keeps it; the register only restyles it (docs/landing-content.md → "The registers" lists every preset and treatment). Text is click-to-edit and photographs Replace-able in the preview editor, and the content paths named are Content-panel slots in `catalog.json`'s `contentContract` (switches such as `home.liveBadge` stay in code).

- **Registers**: `luxe-light` (`glow`) here and on the three original remixes; the restyled remixes wear `wayfinding` (`transit`, care-direct), `bubblegum` (`candy`, care-dental-bright), `inkblot` (`wall-label`, `hairline`, care-psychology-studio) and `groove` (`sleeve`, `grain`, care-therapy-duet); `parlor` (`gilt`, `hairline`, care-primary-magnolia); `trailhead` (`fieldbook`, `hairline`, care-therapy-opentrail); `hearth` (`weave`, care-therapy-kindred); `colophon` (`marginalia`, care-psychology-cambridge); `boreal` (`frost`, care-psychology-northlight); `terrazzo` (`terrazzo`, care-dental-atwater); `picturebook` (`picturebook`, care-dental-fernfinch); `seaglass` (`seaglass`, care-obgyn-solana); `adobe` (`framestack`, `tintbands`, care-obgyn-oakadobe); `fogline` (`framestack`, `inkover`, care-obgyn-clearwater).
- **Hero** (`split-media`, or `full-bleed-media` when `home.layout` is `"full-bleed"`; `home.accent` picks the accent placement): the computed open/closed `badge` (not content; `home.liveBadge` wears it as the live status pill), `credit` (`home.credit`), `aside` `directory` (`home.directory.items[]` label/note/icon/path; `lines` for plain lines; rendered once it has items), `seal` on the restyled remixes.
- **Prices**: `pricing` `price-list` from `menu` (`menu.groups[].items[]` name/note/price/qualifier; every line is editable as `groups.<g>.items.<i>.<field>`). Without a price list the home carries the insurance strip straight under the hero; with one, coverage follows the prices.
- **The `stack` home** (`home.stack`, set by care-obgyn-oakadobe and care-obgyn-clearwater): the hero photograph with the headline over it and no ask of its own (`home.headline`, `home.hero`), one band line (`home.stack.band`), the frames edge to edge at one size with a caption band under each (`home.stack.frames[]` image/caption, "" for none), the outcomes report as stats `bars` when it has rows (`home.stack.report` kicker/intro/footnote/note and `rows[]` label/value; the `note` is the visible tag that marks demo figures as sample data), and the closing line over the booking link (`home.stack.closing`, `home.stack.closingCta`). The team, services, price list, coverage strip, and booking keep their own pages; unset, the home is the clinic home below.
- **Home bands**: `promises`, `exhibits` and `spotlight` exports feed the icon list, exhibit cards and the spotlight split. `exhibits.layout` picks how the exhibits print: `plates` (the default, showcase `card-grid`, `meta` as the eyebrow) `notes` (showcase `stories`, `meta` as the byline that opens each note), or `directory` (showcase `filterable-grid` of the resolved providers themselves — name, photo, credentials and role as the eyebrow, each provider's optional `tags` line — " · " between tags — its filter chips — so a Manage edit to the providers repaints the home as it does /providers; `exhibits.extras` adds each card's blurb by `providerId` and `items` stays empty; a directory is the practice's people, so the home drops its portraits band for it). `spotlight.report` (label, title, summary, `rows[]` label/value, signature, stamp) turns the spotlight into a content-split `report` — a sample document where the photograph would sit, the spotlight's bullets beside it — and, once its title is filled, moves the spotlight up to lead the home ahead of the journey; leave the title empty for the photograph split in its usual place. The base and the original remixes ship them empty (the service grid leads), with the slots in place for an owner to fill.
- **Journey**: `journey` (`kicker`, `title`, `layout`, `steps[]` label/title/description/image) feeds a `steps` band after the home bands — `timeline` (photographic once any step has an image), `horizontal-rail`, or `numbered-cards` by `layout`. Empty steps drop the band; the base and the earlier remixes ship it empty.
- **Under the hero**: `home.progress` (before/after pairs — `gallery` `before-after`, each pair's `beforeLabel`/`afterLabel` on the chips) and `home.journey` (the patient's path — `steps` `horizontal-rail`, each step with an `icon` pictogram or an `image` plate) hang straight under the hero when a seed fills them; `home.layout: "full-bleed"` sets the hero photograph full-bleed (`full-bleed-media`). care-dental-atwater wears all three; care-dental-fernfinch hangs its storybook (five `image` pages, numbered by `label`) under a full-bleed hero, and an unheaded `promises` list (empty `kicker` and `title`) renders as a strip of facts with no heading. care-obgyn-solana leads with `exhibits` as stage cards (`showcase` `card-grid`): each item's `icon` sets its emblem, `points` its short list, and an empty `meta` drops the eyebrow.

## Agent recipe: make it yours

- Change the practice: edit `practice`, `home`, `story`, and `booking` in `web/app/src/View/Care/content.ts`. `landingCopy` carries the nav labels and page copy that name the kind of practice, so a seed swap retrades the whole site.
- Change the home's argument: fill or empty `promises`, `exhibits`, `journey`, `menu`, `spotlight`, and `faq` — an empty one drops its section (and the catalog's `landing.pages` must match; the fidelity test says so).
- Change the facts: edit `codePractice` (providers, services, insurance, location/hours, reviews, new-patient guide) and `clinicHours`. Hours are numbers — `day` is the weekday index (0 = Sunday), `open`/`close` are minutes since midnight; the live badge follows.
- Change the appointment offering: edit `codeAppointments` — visit types carry the slot length they book (5–240 minutes), providers carry weekly windows (`day`/`start`/`end`, minutes since midnight). Slots pack back-to-back from each window's start; the workspace widget and a deploy project identical slots.
- Swap in real photographs: run each original through `npm run image -- responsive <file> --out-dir web/app/public/care --alt "..."` and paste the emitted entry into the matching slot. Keep the art direction: calm natural light, unposed, warm — nothing stocky, nothing fluorescent, and no red cross emblem (it is a protected symbol).
- Content tests guard the file: contract-shaped exports parse contract-clean, hours and windows stay inside a day, ids match the contract grammar, every image carries dimensions + alt text, and the booking copy stays free of clinical asks.

## Non-goals for this pack

- Clinical intake of any kind — symptoms, reasons for visit, health history (deliberate architecture, see above)
- Payments, reminders, or waitlists (booking-domain upgrades, not template features)
- Runtime review ingestion — reviews are owner-curated contract data
- Patient portals or accounts — the shipped site is fully client-side; booking rides the platform's managed booking domain
