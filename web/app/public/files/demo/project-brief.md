# Harbor Rebrand — project brief

**Client:** Harbor Supply Co.
**Due:** end of quarter
**Owner:** studio

## The ask

Refresh the wordmark and packaging system without losing the shelf
recognition the navy band built. Deliverables: logo suite, palette,
label grid, and a one-page usage sheet.

## Constraints

- Print first: the labels run on uncoated stock, two spot colors.
- The wordmark must survive at 12 mm wide (the tin lid).
- Keep the whale. Everyone keeps trying to remove the whale. Keep it.

## Milestones

1. Moodboards and palette (see Design folder)
2. Wordmark rounds
3. Label grid + dielines
4. Usage sheet and handoff
