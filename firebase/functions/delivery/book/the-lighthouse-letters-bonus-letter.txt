While the signed hardcover makes its way to you, here is a letter that did
not make the final edition.

    Dear M.,

    The keeper before me left three rules pinned above the lamp: trim the
    wick before you are tired, log the weather before you forget it, and
    never write letters during a storm. I have kept the first two
    faithfully. This letter is my apology for the third.

    The light turned green again last night — the whole beam of it, for
    four sweeps, the way you never believed me about. I have started
    keeping the log in pencil so the sea air cannot smudge what I saw.

    Come in spring. The stairs are seventy-eight now; I counted them
    twice.

        — E.
